The Story Behind Earthquake Tracker

How a simple coding exercise evolved into a feature-rich, live seismic radar.

🌱 The Spark: How It All Started

  • A Humble Beginning: The project started while watching an introductory Flutter & API tutorial that demonstrated basic HTTP fetching of public USGS earthquake data.
  • The Spark of Curiosity: While the tutorial covered the basics of rendering a plain list, it sparked an obsession: why stop at a basic list when seismic data can be interactive, visual, and life-saving?
  • Breaking Past the Tutorial: Rather than leaving it as a finished class project, curiosity drove a complete redesign from the ground up to explore what a full-fledged monitoring app could look like.

🚀 Embracing New Elements & Advanced Features

  • Tectonic Plate & Fault Line Overlays: Integrated interactive geospatial boundary lines so users can visualize why and where over 80% of major earthquakes originate along shifting plates.
  • Critical Hazard Markers: Expanded beyond standard quake pins to include active volcano overlays, global nuclear power plant locations, and real-time tsunami warnings.
  • Custom Proximity & Precision Filters: Added dual minimum/maximum magnitude range sliders and location-based personal Home Pin drop to calculate exact distances to epicenters.
  • Magnitude-Scaled Haptics: Programmed dynamic vibration feedback so users can tactilely experience the earthquake's relative intensity right on their device.

✨ Designing a Better User Experience

  • Refined Spacing & Balance: Replaced cluttered screens with breathable card layouts, balanced grids, and harmonious typography for effortless readability during critical events.
  • Dark Mode Aesthetic: Built on a deep, minimal black palette designed for comfortable viewing and sharp visual clarity.
  • Frictionless Interaction: Engineered smooth radar map panning, quick-toggle overlays, and fluid gestures so critical seismic insights are always accessible within a single tap.
